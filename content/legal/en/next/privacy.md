## 2. What we collect, and why

### On this website

- **Preferences.** Your theme and cookie choices are stored in your own browser. They never reach us.
- **Analytics, only if you accept them.** With your consent, Google Analytics measures visits and how the site is used. You can withdraw consent at any time through *Cookie settings* in the footer.
- **Enterprise inquiries.** If you use the contact form, we receive your name, work email, company, topic and message, only to answer you.

### The waitlist

- Your **company's name and the address it would like**, your **name, email, what you want to prototype, your language**, and the date and fact of your **consent**.
- Your email is confirmed by a link we send. Until you confirm it, we send nothing else.
- Purpose: to consider your company's request, open its trial and write to you about it.

### Your account

- You sign in with your **email address and a password** you choose, or with **GitHub**. Of a password we keep only a salted hash, never the password itself; when you set one, we check it against known breaches through Have I Been Pwned, sending only the first five characters of a SHA-1 hash of it, never the password nor anything that names you. If you turn on **two-step sign-in**, we keep its secret, sealed, and your recovery codes as hashes. With GitHub, we keep only your **verified email, your name and GitHub's identifier** for your account, never your GitHub password.
- If your company's admin invites you, we receive your **email address** from them, to send you the invitation.
- We record **which company account you belong to and whether you are its admin**, the **state of your invitation** (invited, active, expired) and its dates, and **which version of the terms you accepted and when**.
- A session cookie keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary and is not used for anything else.

### What you create

- Your **messages, the documents and images you upload, the prototypes, the builder's notes and the requirements** generated from them. They belong to your company's account and its people, as the [terms of use](/legal/terms/) say.
- Purpose: to run your company's account and its plan. When your company asks for the assisted start, our team reads the prototype and its requirements to write a scope and to do the work the accepted scope describes.
- **Usage records** — how many runs you made, how long they took and what they cost — without any of the content. Purpose: to apply the trial's budget and each plan's limits fairly, and to understand the cost of the platform.

### Paid plans and payments

- When your company chooses a plan, we record the **plan, the state of its subscription** (for example active, past due, cancelled) and its dates, and **the identifier Stripe gives your company as its customer**.
- Stripe, our payment provider, collects the **company's billing details** (its name, billing email and address, and its tax number if it gives one), its **payment method** and its **invoices**. Card details are entered on Stripe's own pages and never reach us.
- Purpose: to sell and bill the plans, to give your company the access it paid for, and to keep the accounting records the law requires.

### The assisted start

- For each order we record **the prototype and the company it is tied to, each version of the scope we write, who accepted which version and when, the state of the payment, and who authorised production and when**.
- Purpose: to agree the work with your company, to do it, and to show later what was agreed and authorised.

### Your company's AI provider key

- On a paid plan, your company's admin gives us the company's own **API key** for its AI provider. We keep the key in a secret store and use it only to run your company's Prototype Builder. Beside it we record its **provider, its last four characters, the date it was checked and its state**.
- Purpose: to run the builder on your company's own provider account, as the [terms of use](/legal/terms/) say.

### Security

- To protect the platform against abuse (for example, automated sign-ups and repeated access attempts), we and our providers process your **IP address and technical data about your browser**, for rate limiting, a human-verification check (Cloudflare Turnstile) and firewall rules.
- We keep an **audit record** of significant actions (an invitation, a sign-in, an acceptance, a deletion, a hand-over): who, when and what, never the content.

## 3. Legal bases

We are established in Portugal, so the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Portuguese law that implements it apply; if you live elsewhere, your local law may give you further rights (for example, the LGPD in Brazil). We rely on:

- **your consent** — for the waitlist contact and for analytics; you can withdraw it at any time, which does not affect the lawfulness of what was done before you withdrew it;
- **the performance of the terms your company accepts**, or steps you ask us to take before them — to run the trial and the paid plans, to bill them, and to scope and carry out an assisted start;
- **our legitimate interest** — to keep the platform and its users secure and to prevent abuse;
- **legal obligations** — to keep invoices and accounting records, and where the law otherwise requires us to keep or disclose data.

To ask for the trial we need your name, your email address, your company's name, the address it wishes for and a few words (at least 10 characters) about what you want to prototype; without them we cannot consider the request. To use the platform, you sign in with your email address and a password, or with GitHub. To pay for a plan or an order, Stripe needs the details its payment pages ask for.

## 4. What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your content to train AI models. We do not use it for advertising. We do not read your prototypes unless your company asks for the assisted start or you ask for our help. We never read your company's AI provider key back to show it to anyone.

## 5. Who processes your data for us

We use the following providers, each under contract and only for the purpose stated:

- **During the trial, our AI model provider (currently Anthropic)** — receives what you write and upload, and the prototype's files, to generate the prototype and the requirements.
- **Amazon Web Services** — hosts the platform and its data in the United States, and keeps each company's AI provider key in its secret store.
- **Stripe** — takes the payments for the plans and the assisted start, and keeps the company's billing details, its payment methods and its invoices. **[Counsel: Stripe also acts as an independent controller for some of its processing (for example, fraud prevention and its own legal obligations); confirm how to describe it here, and name the Stripe entity BeeverForge contracts with.]**
- **Resend** — sends our emails (confirmation, invitation, password links, notices) and delivers Enterprise inquiries to us.
- **Cloudflare** — the human-verification check on our forms (it sees your IP address and browser signals) and the service that receives Enterprise inquiries.
- **Google** — website analytics, only if you consent.
- **GitHub** — sign-in, if you choose GitHub; and the hosting of this website.

**On a paid plan, your company's own AI provider** (for now, Anthropic) receives what you write and upload, and the prototype's files, through your company's own key and under the terms your company agreed with it. Your company chooses that provider and is its customer. **[Counsel: the role of the company's own AI provider under the GDPR — the company's processor, or a sub-processor engaged through BeeverForge — and what BeeverForge must say about it.]**

When you set a password, we also ask Have I Been Pwned, a public service we use without an account or a contract, whether it is a known breached one. It receives the first five characters of a SHA-1 hash of the password, from our server: no personal data.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Unconfirmed waitlist entries:** 7 days.
- **Waitlist and invitation records:** 12 months after our last contact with you, or until you ask us to delete them.
- **The account's prototypes, uploads and requirements:** 90 days after the last activity in your company's account, or sooner if they are deleted. While the company has a paid plan, they stay with its account. A payment state never deletes them. **[Product owner and counsel: the period for an account that paid and is now read-only.]**
- **Records of the terms you accepted:** as long as we may need to show what you accepted.
- **Subscription records, invoices and other billing records:** as long as Portuguese tax and accounting law requires. **[Accountant: the period.]**
- **Assisted start orders, their scopes, acceptances and authorisations:** as long as the order, the work and any claim about them need them. **[Counsel: the period.]**
- **Your company's AI provider key:** while your company uses it; it is replaced when the admin enters a new one. **[Product owner: how long a key no longer in use, and its last four characters and dates, are kept once the plan ends or the account is deleted.]**
- **Audit and usage records** (no content): 2 years.
- **Security and server logs:** 30 days.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask to **access, correct or delete** your data, to **receive it in a portable format or have it sent to another provider**, to **know who we share it with**, to **withdraw your consent**, to **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest and to ask us to **restrict** it. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from your account page, or by writing to contact@beeverforge.com; billing records the law requires us to keep stay for the period it sets. You may also complain to a data protection authority — in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), or the authority where you live.

## 9. Security

The platform runs behind a firewall, signs people in with a password (at least 12 characters, checked against known breaches when it is set, kept only as a salted hash) or with GitHub, offers two-step sign-in with an authenticator app — required for BeeverForge's team —, limits repeated attempts, keeps every run of the AI model in an isolated environment, encrypts data in transit, and keeps personal data out of its technical logs. A company's AI provider key is written to a secret store that the hub never reads back, and card details never reach us. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Children

BeeverForge is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
