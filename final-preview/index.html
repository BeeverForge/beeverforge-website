From idea to engineered software01 / Product, portfolio, engineering

Beyond a coding assistant

More throughput, without developer-written code.

Even with an LLM assistant, manual development still depends on people to turn each answer into code, checks and a reviewed change. BeeverForge orchestrates LLM agents across that execution workflow. Human developers do not need to write code; they can focus on requirements, architecture, quality and approval.

This approach is designed to increase development throughput and reduce lost context and rework. Outcomes depend on the project, configuration and review process; no fixed productivity gain is implied.

Frequently asked questions

Choose with clarity.

Will Community remain open source?

Yes. The Apache-2.0 engineering core remains available to run, inspect and contribute to. Enterprise is the paid edition built around that foundation.

Is Enterprise available now?

Yes. BeeverForge Enterprise is available for commercial adoption. Contact BeeverForge to discuss your organization’s requirements and pricing.

What does Enterprise add?

Enterprise adds Prototype Builder, the requirements handoff, Portfolio Manager, Virtual Architect, SSO, RBAC and BeeverForge commercial support around the engineering core.

How does the Product Owner fit?

The Product Owner (PO) receives consolidated requirements and routes prepared work to the existing execution flow. The Community core also documents an optional Product Owner workflow. The role’s human or automated operation depends on implementation.

Who decides what reaches production?

The documented core uses configurable policies. Independent review is advisory by default, and production promotion requires human approval.

Does open source mean zero operating cost?

No. The Community code is Apache-2.0, while coding models, infrastructure and external services may have separate costs.

Do developers need to write code?

No. LLM agents write and revise the code in the BeeverForge workflow. People still define requirements, guide architecture, review evidence and approve production under their policies.
