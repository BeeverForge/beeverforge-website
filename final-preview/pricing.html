You stay in control

Your tokens, your infrastructure, your data.

The monthly fee gives access to the platform. Infrastructure, AI consumption and third-party services are paid separately by the customer.

You pay your AI provider and your cloud directly, at their prices, with nothing marked up by us. BeeverForge charges for the software and the platform, never for tokens or for hosting your apps.

Prototype runs on OpenCode, which supports dozens of model providers. After the trial, it runs on your own key, with the provider you prefer: for example Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Qwen or Kimi, or a model you run yourself. During the trial, BeeverForge’s key covers it.

In the factory, bring the coding agents you already use: Claude Code, Codex (OpenAI) and OpenCode, with Kimi in preview, chosen per role and per model. Through OpenCode, use the model provider you prefer, including open models such as Qwen and models running on your own machines; OpenCode is proven end to end with Amazon Bedrock.

Frequently asked questions

Before you choose.

How do we start?

Ask for your company’s trial. BeeverForge approves companies a few at a time; once your company’s space is open, its admin subscribes to Prototype from the company’s billing page. For Business or Enterprise, write to contact@beeverforge.com.

Does the trial turn into a paid plan?

No. Nothing is charged unless your company subscribes. When the fifteen days end, new work stops, except generating, once, the requirements of a prototype you ask BeeverForge’s architects to look at, and your people can still sign in to read and download what they made.

Which AI model does Prototype use?

During the trial, BeeverForge’s key covers it. After the trial, Prototype runs on your company’s own key, with the model provider it prefers: for example Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Qwen or Kimi, or a model you run yourself. Your provider bills you directly, and BeeverForge sells no AI credits.

Is the price per person?

No. Prototype is priced per company, not per seat.

Can we cancel Prototype?

Yes, from your company’s billing page. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period already paid.

What happens if a payment fails?

Your company keeps its access for a grace period, with a notice. After that, the account becomes read-only until the payment is settled. No data is deleted because of a payment; the retention rules of the privacy policy still apply.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices are in euros and exclude VAT, which is added where applicable.

Is hosting or an SLA included?

We never host your apps or charge for hosting them: with Business and Enterprise, they run in your own infrastructure. An SLA is offered only with Enterprise, as an option.

Do we need the assisted start?

No, it is optional. A team with its own engineers can take the design and run the factory on its own. If you want BeeverForge’s architects alongside you, asking is free, and you pay only if you accept their proposal.
