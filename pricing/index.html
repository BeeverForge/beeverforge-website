How the assisted start works ↓

Prices are per company, not per seat, in euros. They exclude VAT, which is added where applicable. Your company subscribes and pays from its own account, at its own address.

What the fee covers

What you pay us, and what you pay others.

The monthly fee gives access to the platform. Infrastructure, AI consumption and third-party services are paid separately by the customer.

After the trial, your company brings its own AI provider key — for now, an Anthropic API key — and the provider bills your company directly for what it uses. BeeverForge sells no AI credits, and its own key never stands in for yours. Without a valid key, your account can still read and export, but starts no new runs.

The trial is the one exception: for fifteen days, BeeverForge covers a limited AI and infrastructure budget.

Plainly

What a plan includes, and what it does not.

A price should not promise more than it gives.

Prototype includes

  • Access to the platform at your company’s own address
  • Your data, readable and exportable whatever the state of a payment, for as long as the privacy policy’s retention rules keep it
  • A billing page to update payment details, see invoices and cancel

No plan includes

  • Hosting your product or running it in production
  • Infrastructure, AI consumption or third-party services
  • Unlimited support
  • A service level agreement (SLA)
  • Production as part of the assisted start’s base price

The assisted start

Asking is free. You pay only if you accept the proposal.

€1,490 + VAT where applicable, for the base package. It is tied to one prototype and to your company, and the work starts after payment.

  1. 01

    Ask from your prototype

    Free

    When a prototype shows what you mean, ask BeeverForge’s team to look at it, from the Prototype Builder. Asking costs nothing and commits neither side.

  2. 02

    Talk it through

    Free

    BeeverForge’s team reviews the prototype and its requirements and talks them through with you, at no cost.

  3. 03

    We send a proposal

    If you want to go forward

    BeeverForge’s team sends you a proposal with a written scope: what the base package delivers, and what it does not. Each proposal has a version.

  4. 04

    You accept, then pay

    €1,490 + VAT where applicable

    Your company’s admin accepts the version your company agrees with; nothing is due before that. The work starts once the payment is confirmed. Paying does not mean the work is done.

  5. 05

    We do the work and show it to you

    Within the accepted scope

    BeeverForge’s team works within the scope you accepted and shows you the result before production, for you to accept.

  6. 06

    You authorise production

    Only you can

    Nothing reaches production without your company’s explicit authorisation, given by a person. A payment, an automated message or the virtual PO cannot give it.

Anything beyond the accepted scope is quoted separately, outside the platform, and starts only once you agree to it. Infrastructure, AI consumption and third-party services stay your company’s own.

Frequently asked questions

Before you choose.

How do we start?

Ask for your company’s trial. BeeverForge approves companies a few at a time; once your company’s space is open, its admin subscribes to Prototype from the company’s billing page.

Is the price per person?

No. Prices are per company, not per seat: one subscription covers your whole company.

Does the trial turn into a paid plan?

No. Nothing is charged unless your company subscribes. When the fifteen days end, new work stops, except generating, once, the requirements of a prototype you ask BeeverForge’s team to look at, and your people can still sign in to read and download what they made.

Why do we bring our own AI provider key?

Because AI consumption is your company’s, paid to your provider at its prices. After the trial, your company’s admin enters the company’s own API key — for now, an Anthropic API key — in its account. BeeverForge keeps it in a secret store, shows you only its last four characters, and sells no AI credits.

Can we cancel?

Yes, from your company’s billing page. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period already paid.

What happens if a payment fails?

Your company keeps its access for a grace period, with a notice. After that, the account becomes read-only until the payment is settled. No data is deleted because of a payment; the retention rules of the privacy policy still apply.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices are in euros and exclude VAT, which is added where applicable.

Is hosting or support included?

No plan includes hosting your product, a service level agreement (SLA) or unlimited support.

Does it cost anything to ask BeeverForge’s team?

No. Asking from your prototype, and talking it through with BeeverForge’s team, is free. If you want to go forward, the team sends a proposal with the scope; you pay the assisted start’s base package only if you accept it, and the work starts after payment.

What does the assisted start include?

What its accepted proposal says. Anything beyond its scope is quoted separately. Nothing reaches production without your explicit authorisation.
