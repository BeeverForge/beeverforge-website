Plainly

What the trial is, and what it is not.

A trial should not promise more than it gives. This is what fifteen days with the Prototype Builder include.

The trial includes

  • Your company’s own space at its own address, with its own Prototype Builder
  • A clickable prototype on simulated data, with the roles of your product to switch between
  • The requirements: capabilities, decisions, rules, open questions and a glossary, in your language
  • Your own documents as material for the conversation: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text and images
  • Inviting your colleagues and sharing prototypes with them
  • A download of the requirements package, yours to keep

The trial does not

  • Build, host or publish your real product
  • Connect to your systems or use your real data: prototypes run on fictitious data
  • Turn into a charge: nothing is billed unless your company chooses a plan
  • Keep your work indefinitely: unless your company continues on a paid plan, prototypes are deleted after the retention period
  • Commit you or BeeverForge to a project: asking BeeverForge’s architects is free, and the assisted start is a separate order, paid only if you accept their proposal

Your data

Used for the trial, and nothing else.

What you write and upload is used only to run the trial: it is sent to our AI model provider to build the prototype and the requirements, and we do not sell it or use it to train models. Prototypes and requirements are deleted ninety days after the last activity in your company’s account, unless it continues on a paid plan, and you can delete everything sooner from your account page. Please use fictitious data and do not upload confidential material or other people’s personal data.

Frequently asked questions

Before you join.

Who can ask for the trial?

A company. A person asks for it on the company’s behalf; BeeverForge reviews each request and, when it approves one, opens the company’s own space with its own Prototype Builder. The person who asked becomes the account’s admin.

Is the trial free?

Yes, within your company’s quota, for the fifteen days of the trial. No card is asked for, and the trial never turns into a charge: nothing is billed unless your company chooses a plan.

Can my colleagues take part?

Yes. The account’s admin invites them; each of them signs in with their email address and a password, or with GitHub, and accepts the terms. Everyone works in your company’s space, within its quota.

What happens when we reach the quota?

The builder tells you when you can continue. Each company’s trial has its own quota, so every company in the trial gets a fair share of the capacity.

Can I make more than one prototype?

One per person in your company’s account, about one product. It can change as much as the quota allows — screens, rules, data, look — but it stays that product: a request for a different product is declined. Your colleagues each make their own. When it is ready, generate its requirements.

Which address will our space have?

The one you ask for, followed by .beeverforge.com — for example acme.beeverforge.com. It is a wish: BeeverForge may change it when it sets up the space, and some names are reserved.

What happens after the fifteen days?

The trial ends: new work stops, except generating, once, the requirements of a prototype you ask BeeverForge’s architects to look at, and nothing is charged. To continue, your company subscribes to Prototype — the same account, at the same address, with its data — and brings its own AI provider key. If it does not, its people can still sign in to read and download their prototypes and requirements until they are deleted. See the plans and prices ↗

Do we need our own AI provider key?

Not during the trial: BeeverForge’s key covers it. After it, yes: Prototype runs on your company’s own key, with the model provider it prefers — for example Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Qwen or Kimi, or a model you run yourself — and your company pays the provider directly for what it uses. BeeverForge sells no AI credits.

Is the prototype my product?

No. It is a way to see the idea and correct it on simulated data. The requirements are what a team builds the real product from: BeeverForge’s, if you ask for the assisted start, or your own.

Who can see my prototypes?

You, and the colleagues you share them with. BeeverForge reads your prototype and its requirements only when you ask its architects to look at it or ask for our help. The AI model provider processes what you write in order to build the prototype: ours during the trial, your company’s own after it.

Can I bring my own documents?

Yes: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text and images, as material for the conversation. During the trial, do not upload confidential documents or other people’s personal data.

What does going to production involve?

Conclude your prototype and ask BeeverForge’s architects to look at it: asking and talking it through are free. If you want to go forward, the architects send a proposal with the scope, and you pay the assisted start’s base package, €1,490 + VAT where applicable, only if you accept it. The work starts after payment, and anything beyond the scope is quoted separately. Nothing reaches production without your explicit authorisation.
