A requirements generator, not a site builder

A prototype to see. Requirements to build.

The prototype is how you see the idea and correct it quickly; it runs in your browser on simulated data and is never the product. What BeeverForge takes to production are the requirements written alongside it. When you hand them over, our portfolio manager reads them against what already exists, BeeverForge provisions the environment, and we come back to you with a proposal. Nothing you prototype is published as a website or connected to your systems.

Plainly

What the trial is, and what it is not.

A trial should not promise more than it gives. This is what fifteen days with the Prototype Builder include.

The trial includes

  • A clickable prototype on simulated data, with the roles of your product to switch between
  • The requirements: capabilities, decisions, rules, open questions and a glossary, in your language
  • Your own documents as material for the conversation: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text and images
  • Sharing a prototype with other people taking part in the trial
  • A download of the requirements package, yours to keep

The trial does not

  • Build, host or publish your real product
  • Connect to your systems or use your real data: prototypes run on fictitious data
  • Keep your work indefinitely: prototypes are deleted after the retention period
  • Commit you or BeeverForge to a project: going to production is a separate conversation

Your data

Used for the trial, and nothing else.

What you write and upload is used only to run the trial: it is sent to our AI model provider to build the prototype and the requirements, and we do not sell it or use it to train models. Prototypes and requirements are deleted ninety days after your last activity, and you can delete everything sooner from your account page. Please use fictitious data and do not upload confidential material or other people’s personal data.

Frequently asked questions

Before you join.

Is the trial free?

Yes, within a quota per person, during the fifteen days of your invitation. No card is asked for.

What happens when I reach the quota?

The builder tells you when you can continue. The quota exists so that everyone in the trial gets a fair share of the capacity.

What happens after the fifteen days?

You can still sign in to read and download your prototypes and requirements until they are deleted. Starting new work needs a new invitation.

Is the prototype my product?

No. It is a way to see the idea and correct it on simulated data. The requirements are what a team builds the real product from: BeeverForge’s, if you choose to go to production with us, or your own.

Who can see my prototypes?

You, and the people you share them with. BeeverForge reads your requirements only when you hand them over. Our AI model provider processes what you write in order to build the prototype.

Can I bring my own documents?

Yes: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text and images, as material for the conversation. During the trial, do not upload confidential documents or other people’s personal data.

What does going to production involve?

You hand the requirements to BeeverForge. Our portfolio manager reads them, BeeverForge provisions the environment, and we come back to you with a proposal. There is no obligation, and nothing is built without your agreement.
