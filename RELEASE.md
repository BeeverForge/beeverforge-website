# Multilingual site and theme release

## Product claims

- The public Community code and documentation support LLM-agent execution from prepared work, configured checks, independent review, merge policies and a human production gate. The copy explicitly says developers do not need to write code in this workflow while people retain product, architecture, quality and production decisions.
- The productivity comparison describes the difference between manual coding with LLM assistance and orchestrated agent execution. No measured or guaranteed productivity figure is claimed.
- Enterprise availability and the availability of SSO, RBAC, Prototype Builder, Portfolio Manager, Virtual Architect and BeeverForge support were confirmed by the product owner in this conversation. No protocol, SLA, hosting model or price was invented.

## Verification before deployment

- `node scripts/build.mjs` generated `/`, `/es/`, `/pt/` and localized thank-you pages.
- Chromium local lab review covered all three languages, dark/light, 1440×900 desktop and 390×844 mobile: 12 full-page screenshots, one H1, correct `lang`, canonical and indexability, no page-level horizontal overflow, and form present.
- Direct loads, all internal section anchors, mobile menu and Escape behavior passed on each language.
- Theme persisted through locale change. The contact form success and error paths were tested with mocked HTTP responses; no actual inquiry was sent.
- SEO: self-canonicals, `hreflang` en/es/pt-BR/x-default, localized title/description/OG metadata, sitemap entries for three indexable URLs, `noindex` confirmation and retired-concept pages. No search ranking claim is made.
- In a headless Chromium local-server lab run at 1440×900, `DOMContentLoaded` was 120 ms on the cold first English load, 13 ms in Spanish and 12 ms in Portuguese; `load` was 145, 52 and 48 ms respectively. These localhost timings exclude public-network latency and are not Lighthouse scores or real-user data.

## Deployment

Merge the review branch into `main`. The GitHub Pages workflow builds and publishes the three languages and shared assets. After the workflow succeeds, verify the public URLs, direct reloads, TLS, theme switch, language switch, and Worker response path. Keep the existing domain DNS configuration.

## Hosted company accounts (2026-10-27)

The trial became a hosted company account in its trial plan, by the contract decided on 2026-10-27 between the door, the infrastructure and the site.

- The Try it page, in the three languages, speaks of a trial for a company: the company asks for it, BeeverForge approves it and sets up the company's own space at its own address under beeverforge.com with its own Prototype Builder, the person who asked becomes the account's admin and invites colleagues, the trial lasts fifteen days within the company's quota, and the same account continues on the Business plan, at the same address, with its data. The steps are six (inviting colleagues is one); one prototype, about one product, stays per person within the company's account. The questions add who can ask (a company), colleagues and the address, and the one about the end of the trial says Business. The sign-in hint links to `https://app.beeverforge.com`. The confirmation page says the e-mail address, the company's place on the waitlist and that we write once the request is reviewed.
- The form asks first for the company's name (required, at most 120 characters) and the company's wished address (required), a text field with `.beeverforge.com` beside it, a hint that says the rule and that the address is a wish we may change, and a sentence under the field when the address is malformed or reserved (`aria-describedby` names the hint and the sentence; the field is `aria-invalid` while the sentence shows). The rule is the contract's: `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{1,18}[a-z0-9]$`, no `--`, not ending in `-preview`, not reserved. Capitals and surrounding spaces are tidied, not refused. `assets/try.js` posts `company` and `slug` with the other fields to `https://app.beeverforge.com/waitlist`, which is also the only door in the page's policy; the laptop build's local door stays `http://127.0.0.1:8500/waitlist`.
- The terms, the privacy policy and the acceptable use policy, in the three languages, name `app.beeverforge.com` and the company's own address instead of `try.beeverforge.com`; the trial is offered to a company account; the person who asks becomes its admin and may invite colleagues, each of whom accepts the terms; content belongs to the company's account and its people, who keep the rights they have in it; the account's prototypes, uploads and requirements follow the existing retention (90 days after the last activity in the account) and stay with the account if it moves to Business; the privacy policy lists the company's name and wished address among the waitlist's data, the colleague's e-mail address an admin gives us to invite them, and the account and admin role we record. They stay drafts (`status: draft`, version 0.1, dated 2026-10-27), and every note for counsel is unchanged.
- Checked by `node scripts/build.mjs` (the default build: `try/index.html` carries no `127.0.0.1` and no `data-local-*`) and in headless Chromium against a local static server: the fifteen changed pages (`try/`, `try/thank-you/` and the three legal pages, in three languages) at 1440×900 and 390×844 each answered 200, with one H1, the right `lang`, no horizontal overflow and no console error; served from `127.0.0.1` the default build's form is read-only from load, the new fields too. The open form (reached through a mapped host name with a site key written into the served page, Turnstile stubbed, the door answered by the browser's request interception) refused, in each language and at both widths, `Bad--Name`, `ab`, `1acme`, `acme-` with the format sentence and `admin`, `acme-preview`, `beeverforge` with the reserved sentence, posted nothing while the address was wrong and put the focus on it, cleared the sentence as soon as the address was right, and then posted one `POST https://app.beeverforge.com/waitlist` whose JSON carried `company` and `slug` (`acme-field` from `  Acme-Field `) beside the other fields, before reaching the confirmation page.
